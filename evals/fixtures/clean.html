How Slackwater reads the water

  1. Pick a launch

    Drop a pin or choose a saved put-in. We match it to the closest tide and current stations.

  2. Set your limits

    Tell us the current and wind you are comfortable in. Defaults follow common sea kayak guidance.

  3. Read one chart

    Calm windows are shaded, hazards are flagged in magenta, and the chart updates when the forecast does.
